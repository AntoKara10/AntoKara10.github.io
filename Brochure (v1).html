Tier 2 & 3 Feature

RAG — Red / Amber / Green tracking

Every reporting period, each KPI is compared against the target set at engagement start and assigned a status. No manual interpretation needed — the report tells you where to look.

Red — Act Now
Metric is materially below target or past a defined threshold. Immediate attention required.
Amber — Watch
Metric is trending toward a problem. Not critical yet, but course-correction may be needed.
Green — On Track
Metric is within acceptable range of target. Continue as planned.
